## Technical contract

The T018 amendment in C5 and source-cited candidate define threshold, float Gaussian coefficients/support, integer/fractional BE arithmetic, source-defined padding, expanded/body mask selection and Gaussian-before-BE ordering. Allocate expanded masks before filtering and keep sources immutable. Existing radius/pass/dimension/transient limits fail deterministically. SBAS does not rescale these device-space values. The owner has explicitly included historical ASS transforms and polygon words needed by the frozen blur corpus. Transform execution follows the C3 amendment; drawing words use the same geometry and mask effects pipeline.

## Expected behavior
